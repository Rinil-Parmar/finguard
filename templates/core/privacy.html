Privacy

How FinGuard handles user data

FinGuard stores only the financial information users choose to enter, such as transactions, budgets, savings goals, uploaded receipts, and account activity history.

The application does not connect to bank accounts and does not use third-party banking APIs. Uploaded receipts are linked to the account that created them.

Sessions and cookies are used for login status, visit tracking, and user history features required for the project demonstration.
