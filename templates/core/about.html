About

Personal finance tracking with simple fraud awareness

FinGuard helps users record income, expenses, receipts, budgets, savings goals, and suspicious transaction alerts in one secure Django application.

The platform does not connect to bank accounts. Users manually enter financial records or upload supporting receipt documents, then review dashboards and alerts generated from their own data.
